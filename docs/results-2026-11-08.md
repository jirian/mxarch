# Flattening results, 2026-11-08 (branch `mxint8-reference`)

Protocol for every row: English Wikipedia (`wikimedia/wikipedia` 20231101.en, first
150 articles ≥ 8,000 chars, `data/text/*.source.json`), 64 disjoint windows of 1,024
tokens (65,472 scored tokens), rounding ties away from zero (microxcaling's default),
all GEMM operands quantized unless "weights only". Runs live in
`/work/cs-471/mixedprecisionarchitects/avant-garde/runs/`. Paired-bootstrap SD of
perplexity normalized to FP32 is 0.02–0.2%.

## Emulator cross-check (job 4860058)
Microsoft microxcaling (commit 7bc4195) MXINT8, block 32, vector ops FP32: GPT-2 Small
perplexity 27.709; our `mxint8`: 27.734 (0.09% apart). Its bfloat16 vector-op recipe
gives nonfinite GPT-2 loss.

## Flattening versus model size (jobs 4859947, 4860082)
| GPT-2 | FP32 | MX9 vs FP32 | flattened vs MX9 | flattened vs MX9, weights only |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Small | 25.96 | +1.11% | +3.72% | +1.27% |
| Medium | 19.32 | +1.15% | +3.32% | +2.15% |
| Large | 16.58 | +0.00% | +0.12% | +0.11% |
| XL | 14.87 | +0.02% | +0.14% | +0.11% |

Context 128 on the same tokens gives the same Small penalty (+3.81%). Truncation instead
of rounding raises every mode's loss. Flattening with one rounding (`mxint8_b16`, FP→flat
directly, as Avant-Garde does for MMA outputs) costs +2.48% vs MX9 for Small, less than
the double-rounded weight path. OCP MXINT8 (block 32) costs +6.82% vs FP32 for Small.
